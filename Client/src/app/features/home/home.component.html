What you get

Know where the money actually goes

ReceiptCave reads every receipt into structured data — items, prices, quantities, categories — so your spending can be counted, compared and charted instead of just filed away.

Line-item scanning

Every product, unit price and quantity is read off the receipt and grouped under the merchant that issued it.

Separate spaces

Keep the household, the business and the holiday apart, so one set of receipts never muddles another.

Share it with your family

Invite your partner, your housemates or the whole family into a space. Everyone adds receipts, everyone sees the same running total.

Analysis and insights

Per-item price history, repeat-purchase totals and monthly breakdowns by category and merchant.
